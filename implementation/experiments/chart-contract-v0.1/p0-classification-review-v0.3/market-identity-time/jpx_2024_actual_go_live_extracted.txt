                                                                                 November 3, 2024
                                                                       Tokyo Stock Exchange, Inc.




         Go-Live Date for the Cash Equity Trading System (arrowhead) Upgrade
                             and Extension of Trading Hours, etc.


Tokyo Stock Exchange, Inc. (TSE) has successfully completed the migration and confirmation
work for the system upgrade of its cash equity trading system “arrowhead,” the ToSTNeT system,
and the Market Information System. The upgraded system will go live on November 5, 2024 (Tue.),
as originally scheduled.


In conjunction with the system upgrade, TSE will extend the trading hours of the cash equity
market by 30 minutes beginning on the go-live date. The trading hours of the derivatives market
will also be changed on the same date in connection with the extension of trading hours of the
cash equity market. In addition, TSE will introduce a closing auction session in the cash equity
market at the end of the afternoon session.


Please refer to the following link for more details regarding the trading hours, etc.
https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/strengthening/index.html
