## Accounting recipes and definition boundary

The retained default has only two financial V elements; it does not establish
a previously approved nine-factor financial recipe. The following explicit
research extension adds the already-defined common dividend yield as the third
financial V perspective; it is not a book formula or an operating adoption.
No individual firm chooses its own available factors. BANK/INSURANCE/BROKER/
MORTGAGE_REIT stay separate reference groups. Tangible B/P remains additional
because including both book variants as separate core factors duplicates book
exposure. Confirmed zero dividends are valid; absent dividend data are null.
