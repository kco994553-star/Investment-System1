UNASSESSED is unknown, not VALID and not necessarily INVALID. A null ref has a
meaningful reason and cannot act as affirmative evidence. A known REJECTED
assessment retains its original numeric diagnostic literal and reason. It is
not silently converted into absent company data. Conflicting, stale, unresolved
or differently typed refs fail structural closure; this does not retroactively
invalidate legacy history or evaluate a new domain policy.

One canonical-byte reference can identify source content, but relational meaning
also matters: an input contract must point to the selected method, a method to
its factor, a method version/fallback/normalizer to that same method, and each
assessment to the same result/context/source closure. A correctly hashed scope
document is not a producer result. Coherently rehashing malformed fixture bodies
therefore does not satisfy these declared relationships.

## Dependency scope, zero and profile isolation

Declare factor-local, related and shared inputs before assessment. Shared
identity/config/source/PIT failure retains its declared affected scope. A
factor-local failure must not acquire a wider axis/whole-system scope without
lineage evidence; unrelated predeclared inventory does not prove a global
failure. No scope is retrospectively removed because a weight became zero or
an observation failed. Cycles, missing scope declarations and unproven
cross-axis equivalence remain unresolved; this phase supplies no failure rubric.

For the same method/context and evidence obligations, zero/profile overlays
preserve method, applicability, requirements, PIT/provenance, assessment and
authority refs. Only separately authorized numeric contribution can change.
The fixture compares immutable obligations; it does not wire WeightOverride or
compute a weight tree. Same-subject context equality prevents in-place
Official/Custom collapse. It does not prohibit a future independently bound
Custom calculation with its own namespace/result identity and authority.
