### P01-G — APPROVED WITH CLARIFICATION

The no-auto-promotion invariant is approved.

Research-display grant, Frozen grant, and Live grant are independent authorization records. One grant does not create or silently satisfy another.

The same human or system authority may issue more than one grant, but each grant requires its own explicit authorization event.

```text
Research-display grant ≠ Frozen grant ≠ Live grant
```

A producer cannot create any of these grants from its own validation PASS.

Track C `SOFTWARE_FROZEN` is not a Frozen grant and not a Live grant. On PR #4 that phrase means C0–C7 software is frozen, real PIT is not run, Holdout is unconsumed, and C8–C10 are not approved.

`DISPLAY_RESEARCH` does not automatically become `DISPLAY_FROZEN`, `DISPLAY_LIVE`, OFFICIAL, VALIDATED, or PROMOTED. Stale or expired data does not automatically fall back to `DISPLAY_RESEARCH`; it becomes `NOT_AVAILABLE`.

Publication does not recalculate score, rank, regime, zone, or portfolio action.
