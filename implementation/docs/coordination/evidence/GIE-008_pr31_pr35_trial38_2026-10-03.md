## 2. Arithmetic decision: independent reproduction (Python 3.11.15, NumPy 2.3.5)

p-values per fixture. "samples" = replicate mean over the n sampled values. "blocks" = `(Σ full block sums + partial)/n` as in the approved simulation's `run()`. "Exact" = rational arithmetic with inputs read as decimal strings or as binary doubles. v1 = the current kernel.

| Fixture | fsum / samples | fsum / blocks | left fold / samples | left fold / blocks | literal NumPy `run()` | exact decimal | exact binary | v1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE4 (n=5, L=2, seed 200) | .10 | .10 | .10 | .10 | .10 | .10 | .15 | .15 |
| FLIP (n=8, L=3, seed 46) | .10 | .10 | .10 | .10 | **.20** | .10 | .10 | .20 |
| LEFT_SUM (n=9, L=4, seed 34) | .35 | .35 | **.40** | .35 | .35 | .35 | .35 | .40 |
| TIE (n=6, L=2, seed 72) | .45 | .45 | **.40** | .45 | .45 | .45 | .45 | .55 |
| Grouping (n=5, L=2, seed 275) | .50 | **.55** | .50 | .50 | .50 | .55 | .50 | .60 |

Reading of the table (facts, not a decision):

- In FLIP, both exact oracles find 2 analytically degenerate replicates. Literal NumPy's cumulative-sum residues make them non-degenerate with tiny variance, so they count as exceedances (p=.20). The approved convention says a degenerate replicate never exceeds.
- Under block grouping, the left fold (Python 3.11 builtin `sum`) agrees with `fsum` on every fixture here. The interpreter dependence shows up only under sample grouping.
- `fsum`/blocks equals the exact-decimal result on all 5 fixtures. Five fixtures are an illustration, not a proof.
- Exact arithmetic is not a neutral referee: decimal and binary readings of the same inputs disagree on CE4 and on the grouping fixture.
- Two independent oracles (the method verifier's and the skeptic's) agree with Codex's traces on 36/36 cells. Literal `run()` reproduces the original `S_plus1` on 7/7 fixtures. NOT_RUN: literal NumPy on Python 3.12/3.13; the historical NumPy environment is unrecoverable.

Whatever is chosen, the decision record has to pin the reducer, the replicate-mean grouping, the block-sum construction and the degeneracy predicate together.
